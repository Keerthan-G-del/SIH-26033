## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Understanding the problem
Agriculture is one of the major sources of livelihood in India. However, farmers often face difficulties in receiving fair prices for their agricultural products because of the involvement of multiple intermediaries in the traditional supply chain.

After harvesting, agricultural products generally pass through several stages, including local traders, wholesalers, distributors and retailers before reaching the final consumers. Each stage can add additional costs to the product price, while farmers may receive only a limited share of the final selling price.

Additionally, farmers often lack direct access to consumers and bulk buyers, reliable market-demand information, efficient transportation facilities and transparent pricing systems. These challenges can result in reduced farmer earnings, increased consumer prices, delays in distribution and unnecessary supply-chain inefficiencies.

Therefore, there is a need for a technology-driven solution that establishes direct communication between farmers and buyers, improves market transparency and optimizes agricultural distribution.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
AgriConnect – AI-Powered Agricultural Marketplace
We propose AgriConnect, a centralized digital marketplace designed to connect farmers and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) directly with consumers, retailers, restaurants and bulk buyers.

The platform will provide a transparent digital environment where farmers can register, list their agricultural products, specify quantities and selling prices, and receive orders directly from interested buyers.

Consumers and bulk purchasers will be able to browse available products, compare prices, place orders and track deliveries through the platform.

The system will integrate Artificial Intelligence (AI) to analyze historical sales and market-demand information, helping farmers understand demand trends and make better selling decisions. It will also provide logistics support through delivery coordination and route optimization to improve the transportation of agricultural products.

The platform aims to reduce unnecessary intermediaries, improve market accessibility, increase transparency and create a more efficient agricultural supply chain.

## Feasibility and Viability
A. Analysis of the feasibility of the idea
The proposed AgriConnect platform is feasible because it can be developed using existing web development frameworks, cloud technologies, database management systems and machine learning tools.

1. Technical Feasibility

The platform can be developed using React.js, Python, FastAPI and PostgreSQL. Existing mapping services can support location tracking and route visualization. Machine learning libraries can be used to develop demand forecasting models.

2. Economic Feasibility

The initial prototype can be developed using open-source technologies and free development tools. Cloud-based free tiers can be used for initial testing, reducing the initial development expenditure.

3. Operational Feasibility

The platform provides a centralized system that farmers, consumers and bulk buyers can access using smartphones or computers. A simple interface and regional language support can improve usability.

4. Scalability Feasibility

The platform can initially be implemented in a selected region with a limited number of farmers and buyers. Its modular architecture allows additional regions, users, products and logistics partners to be added gradually.

## Impact and Benefits
A. Potential impact on the target audience
The proposed AgriConnect platform aims to create positive changes in the agricultural supply chain by improving market accessibility, pricing transparency and distribution efficiency.

1. Impact on Farmers
- Provides direct access to consumers and bulk buyers.
- Reduces dependency on unnecessary intermediaries.
- Helps farmers identify potential buyers.
- Provides market-demand information for better selling decisions.
- Improves transparency in agricultural transactions.
2. Impact on Consumers
- Provides access to agricultural products directly from farmers.
- Offers transparent product pricing.
- Reduces unnecessary distribution costs where possible.
- Provides convenient online purchasing facilities.
- Improves access to fresh agricultural products.
- 3. Impact on Bulk Buyers
- Simplifies bulk purchasing from farmers and FPOs.
- Provides product availability and quantity information.
- Helps restaurants, retailers and supermarkets identify suppliers.
- Reduces procurement difficulties.
